Here were the rules of the 1v1
Hundred Years War 1v1
England vs France
1. No expansion allowed at all
2. War begins when both sides are ready or 5 years after both sides have chosen their country
3. The only units allowed are infantry, artillery, frigates, and tanks (cavalry)
4. 200k Infantry limit, 100 Artillery limit, 1k tank limit, no frigate limit
5. Factories are not allowed (Germany will produce steel and motors you can buy from to make tanks)
6. Only nationalism, non-aligned, and liberalism are allowed as ideologies
